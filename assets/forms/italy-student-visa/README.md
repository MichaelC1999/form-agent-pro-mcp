Italian National (Type D) Student Visa.

Process for a non-EU national who needs to study in Italy for more than 90
days — typically a semester or full academic year. The applicant files the
Italian National (Type D) visa application with the appropriate Italian
Consulate General serving their US state of residence (NYC, LA, SF, Chicago,
Boston, Detroit, Houston, Miami, or Philadelphia jurisdiction).

Suitable when the user is a US-based student who has been accepted by an
Italian host university (full-degree, exchange, or study-abroad semester),
has Universitaly pre-enrollment confirmation, has proof of accommodation in
Italy, has Schengen-compliant health insurance (€30,000 minimum + repatriation
coverage), and can demonstrate sufficient financial support — either personal
or via a sponsor (bank-sponsorship affidavit).

This process is distinct from a short-stay Schengen tourist visa (Type C),
which covers stays up to 90 days and does not authorize study.
