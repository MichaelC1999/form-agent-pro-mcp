# Parental Travel Authorization

> **This document is required only when the visa applicant is a minor (under 18
> years of age at the time of visa application).** Both parents (or legal
> guardians) must sign. Both signatures must be notarized.

**To:** Visa Office, Italian Consulate General of [CONSULATE_CITY]
**Date:** [DATE]

---

We, the undersigned, the parents (or legal guardians) of:

> **Minor Student:** [MINOR_STUDENT_FULL_LEGAL_NAME]
> **Date of Birth:** [MINOR_STUDENT_DATE_OF_BIRTH]
> **Place of Birth:** [MINOR_STUDENT_PLACE_OF_BIRTH]
> **United States Passport Number:** [MINOR_STUDENT_PASSPORT_NUMBER]

hereby grant our **full and unconditional consent** for our minor child to
travel to and reside in the Republic of Italy for the purpose of attending
the following study-abroad program:

> **Program:** [PROGRAM_NAME]
> **Host Institution:** [HOST_INSTITUTION_NAME]
> **Host City:** [HOST_CITY], Italy
> **Program Dates:** [PROGRAM_START_DATE] to [PROGRAM_END_DATE]
> **Accommodation Address in Italy:** [ITALY_ACCOMMODATION_ADDRESS]

We further authorize the named host institution and, where applicable, the
host family or accommodation provider, to act as our local point of contact
for emergencies during our child's stay in Italy.
