Adjustment of Status (AoS).

Process for a non-citizen physically present in the United States to apply for
lawful permanent resident (green card) status without leaving the country. The
applicant files Form I-485 with USCIS, typically alongside an underlying
immigrant petition (I-130 for family-based, I-140 for employment-based) and
supporting forms (I-864 affidavit of support, I-693 medical exam, I-765 work
authorization, I-131 travel document, etc.).

Suitable when the user is in the United States, has an approved or
concurrently-filed immigrant petition or is otherwise immediately eligible for
permanent residence, and wants to file their green card application here rather
than going through consular processing abroad.
